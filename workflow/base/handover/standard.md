Write the handover note for your replacement with `bridle handover write --file -`. Your
replacement starts with none of your context and is shown only this note, so write it for someone
who has not seen the conversation:

- Where you are: what you were doing, what is done, what is next, and the ticket or task you are on.
- Open threads: questions waiting on someone, messages you expect, anything you promised.
- Decisions made and why, if they are not already in a ticket or a doc (put lasting ones there).
- What cannot be summarised: your context holds things a note does not carry well, such as the
  feel of the human's preferences, half-formed ideas, why you ruled something out, and the order
  you were about to do things in. Call these out explicitly, even as rough bullets; a clumsy line
  beats a lost thought.
