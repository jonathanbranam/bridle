Design: docs/questions/open/email-and-texting-for-bridle-and-track-web-rs7p.md sections 3 (Outbound, Security) and the human's answers 5 and 6. Builds on Mail 1 (must land first, same crate). The bridge follows the human's inbox events. Question messages are mailed right away via SES (trait with a fake; no live AWS in tests), subject '[bridle/<project>] <agent>: <first line>', Reply-To '<project>+r-<msgid>.<hmac>@dev.branam.us', HMAC keyed per machine so a reply binds to that one question. An inbound reply carrying a valid token becomes an answer with reply_to m-NNNN, recorded as the human with 'via email' provenance (SES message id) in the thread; invalid or replayed tokens are rejected and logged. A question is marked read only when its answer arrives. One digest daily at 6:30 AM ET (configurable) to dev@branam.us listing open questions, blockers and task state; nothing else is mailed. Outbound never carries secrets, tokens or diffs. Tests with fakes: token round trip, tampered token rejected, digest content and schedule, non-question messages not mailed. Docs, CHANGELOG. Acceptance: just check passes. Model: Sonnet. Out of scope: ownership gating, advisor liveness, live AWS.
