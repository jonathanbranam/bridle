Goal: the exploration findings doc's frontmatter is checked, and `bridle explore` manages its status (docs/design/explorations.md).

Frontmatter (YAML at top of design/explore/<id>/*.md): exploratory: true, task: <id>, diverges-from: [ids], status: open|concluded|adopted|abandoned.

Do: (1) crates/bridle-spec/src/explore.rs parses and validates it (required keys, status set, diverges-from ids look like g-/a-/r-/s- ids), shared Diagnostic form; (2) `bridle explore check [paths...]` (default design/explore, local), exit 1 on errors; (3) `bridle explore new <id>` scaffolds design/explore/<id>/findings.md with status open (refuses to overwrite); `explore conclude|abandon <id>` rewrite only the status line (rest byte-for-byte). Skip `adopt` (needs the arch-revision flow). Docs: explorations.md, cli.md.

Acceptance: `just check` passes; tests for valid, missing key, bad status, status rewrite preserving the rest. Model: Sonnet.

Out of scope: creating tasks, adopt, merge restrictions on exploration code. Touches lib.rs/cli.rs: run after the arch-list task and after the explore-rules task (same design doc).
