From orchestrator-state.md 'Findings not yet ticketed': a worker (deflake) started two loops of 40 full test runs, pushing load average to 104. Disowned background processes escape bridle's stop cleanup and slow everyone down.

Fix: .bridle/roles/worker.md should require that any background process a worker starts is bounded (e.g. --test-threads 4, no open-ended loops), cleaned up before the turn ends, and never disowned (no nohup/disown/setsid-detach patterns that survive the agent).
