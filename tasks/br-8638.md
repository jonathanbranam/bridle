A separate, durable queue owned by the PM: ordered tiers of equally ranked task IDs on the state branch. Dependencies only for real dependencies. The manager takes from the highest tier with a startable task and picks within a tier by load. bridle queue shows in-progress and next-up. No briefs by message (human, 2026-09-28).
