Read docs/design/human-web-ui.md sections 1 and 5 first. Goal: in crates/bridle-gateway, find every defined daemon the way 'bridle --project' does (~/.bridle/daemons/<project>.json, [projects] and [machines] in ~/.bridle/config.toml; reuse bridle_api::machines, don't reimplement), query them concurrently with a short timeout, and merge; a daemon or project that doesn't answer is reported as unreachable in the result, never an error. Expose as an internal function plus a route under /api/v1 (e.g. GET /api/v1/projects: each project, machine, reachable yes/no). Tests with fake daemons: one up, one down, one slow (timeout). Acceptance: just check passes. Model: Sonnet. Migration: none. Out of scope: listing tasks, actions, login, remote tokens (task 9). Depends on gateway 1 (br-1e88).
