While briefing P2 tasks, a long bridle send body was denied by the permission layer even though tk3m (backtick handling) is already fixed. Reproduced: the denial was NOT triggered by length alone, backticks alone, or angle brackets alone (all tested in isolation and worked). It WAS triggered by a specific combination inside one longer message: text ending in workflow-layers.md's (possessive apostrophe) followed later by a quoted phrase like "Rules have ids" section, inside a multi-sentence body. Splitting the message into several shorter sends worked around it every time.

Low priority -- not blocking (workaround is reliable: keep bridle send bodies short and avoid nested quotes after a possessive apostrophe). Worth a look if it recurs often enough to be annoying. See tk3m (docs/questions/resolved/) for the original related fix and its approach.
