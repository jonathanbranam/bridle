The NUC has Unattended-Upgrade::Automatic-Reboot "true" at 04:00 (/etc/apt/apt.conf.d). A reboot there stops anything started by hand (bridle serve, tmux sessions); nothing restarts it. Simplest: on the NUC, sudo sh -c 'echo Unattended-Upgrade::Automatic-Reboot \"false\"\; > /etc/apt/apt.conf.d/52no-auto-reboot' and check with 'apt-config dump | grep Automatic-Reboot'. Updates still install; only the reboot is skipped. Livepatch covers kernel fixes. Undo by deleting that file. Related: ticket t39j (agents and nightly auto reboots).
Finish with `bridle task done <this task>`.
