2. 'bridle doctor' runs 'claude auth status' in its own environment (verify the command exists with 'claude auth --help' first; if it doesn't, say so in the summary and skip) and reports not-logged-in loudly, with the fix from the ticket (start the daemon from a local terminal or tmux, not over SSH, on macOS).
