## Patterns

- **Coordination costs about 7 times what building does.** Everything except workers comes to
  about 53 of the 61 points; the 151 workers together used 8 (median $0.36 a worker, about
  0.05 points a ticket).
- **Product management is paid for twice**: the PdM stand-in advisor (Opus) plus pm-1 (Sonnet)
  come to about 18 points, more than double all the workers.
- **The Opus sessions spend while the human sleeps.** 11 PM to 6 AM ET, about $55 (about 7
  points). Their median statusline report is about $0.035 for every session: what one wake
  costs, re-reading a large cached context.
- **Ticket-watch notices make up most of the wakes.** Bridle's daemon sent 1,520 messages to
  interactive sessions this week, 1,047 of them watch notices: 507 comments; about 370
  routine steps (open -> planned 116, planned -> claimed 103, claimed -> built 92, pending ->
  open 41, planned -> built 15); about 127 lands (`-> integrated`); about 30 dropped or
  reopened. Batching over 60 s would make at most 565 wakes, over 10 min 446. Waking only for
  lands, drops, reopens and questions would cut about 85%. Ticket 3qha.
- **manager-2 polls**: 637 turns at about $0.05 each, 4 points.
- **Pace** was about 10 points a day (11, 9, 9, 13, 11, 8), heading for about 74% at the
  reset. No five-hour window got past 30%.
- **Hosted agents may count more per dollar than Opus sessions.** A least-squares fit of the
  seven_day gain on Opus and Sonnet cost per bucket (no intercept) gave, in points per dollar:
  6 h buckets, Opus 0.064 / Sonnet 0.233; 12 h, 0.102 / 0.156; 24 h, 0.058 / 0.282. It's noisy
  (1% steps, unmeasured spend), but it points the same way every time. If it holds, hosted
  agents are 25 to 30 points, not 18. It's worth repeating each week (not yet in the script).

## Method and caveats

- `scripts/usage-report.py` reads every local daemon's database (`~/.bridle/daemons/*.json`),
  read-only: hosted `turns` (per-turn cost deltas) and `interactive_usage` (statusline cost
  deltas). A group's share of the week is its share of the measured cost times the seven_day
  points gained, the same estimate as `bridle usage --by name`. Cost is Claude Code's
  list-price figure, used as a relative unit; Anthropic does not publish the limit's weights.
- **Not measured**, so the points above are spread over too little cost and each group is a
  little overstated:
  - the orchestrator since Sun 2026-10-18 09:50 ET, when it moved to dalek-home (about $14 a
    day before, so about 2 points), and the track-web aide. Their statusline posts are refused
    by the daemons that didn't mint the token (ticket 43ka).
  - the human's own sessions (no `external:unbridled` rows this week), the desktop app and
    claude.ai, and other machines' interactive sessions.
- The daily points per dollar (0.10 to 0.16) is the check: a day well above the week's rate
  had spend that wasn't measured (10-19: 0.131 against 0.123 for the week).
- Each project's own `bridle usage` gives the whole week's gain to that project's own spend.
  Use the script's machine-wide figure instead (ticket cgw3).

## What came of it

- Bugs filed and readied: 43ka (statusline posts dropped outside the token's project), h2p5
  (`usage --week` shows UTC times as Eastern and the old week's peak on day 1), 9uqw (every
  hosted row marked "(ext)"), cgw3 (per-project share of the whole gain).
- fpqy (readied): `bridle session stop`, to hand over and stop a session until it is started
  again.
- 3qha (for the human): what each ticket watcher should be woken for. The human: "knowing when
  a ticket lands is usually important. But not for every switch."
- The human's plan (2026-10-19): stop almost every aide and advisor overnight, and more during
  the day; keep one orchestrator per machine.

per dollar should drop slightly.
